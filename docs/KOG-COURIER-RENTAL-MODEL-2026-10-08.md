# Kingdom Organics - Courier Rental Model

Date: 2026-10-08
Prepared for: Kingdom Organics / Hive Health Ecosystem Ltd
Status: planning document, internal

## THE IDEA IN ONE LINE

The courier is a customer. They can rent the vehicle from us by the day, or bring their own and keep more of the per-drop fee. All electric.

---

## PART 1 - THE THREE COHORTS

**What this section is:** who a courier can be, and what each one needs to start.

| Cohort | What they bring | What they need | Fee they keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A - Brings nothing | No vehicle | Rent from us. No KYC needed on the e-bike. | 100 pct |
| B - Brings a bike | Pedal-assist under 25 km/h | KYC only if throttle or motorised | 100 pct |
| C - Brings a car or van | Own vehicle | Full KYC and vehicle record | 100 pct |

**The rule across all three: the courier keeps 100 percent of the per-drop fee. Rent is the only deduction.** That is the simplicity. Nothing else comes off their money.

Why this matters: cohort A turns someone with no capital into a courier the same morning they sign up. Cohort C rewards someone who already has a car without costing us a vehicle.

## PART 2 - THE RENTAL LADDER, ALL ELECTRIC

**What this section is:** the three vehicles on offer, what each costs us, and what we charge per day.

### Tier 1 - Cargo e-bike, L1e-A pedal-assist

- Buy cost: 1,449 EUR each VERIFIED
- Daily rent: **12 EUR**
- Payback: 121 rental days, about 0.5 years at 5 days a week
- Zone it serves: Zone 1, in town
- Licence needed: NONE
- Insurance needed: NONE
- Registration or motor tax: NONE
- Cycle lanes: LEGAL

### Tier 2 - Electric moped, L1e-B

- Buy cost: about 3,000 EUR each ESTIMATE
- Daily rent: **20 EUR**
- Payback: 150 rental days, about 0.6 years at 5 days a week
- Zone it serves: Zone 2, the ring
- Licence needed: AM licence
- Insurance needed: Yes

### Tier 3 - Electric van, N1

- Lease cost to us: about 13.16 EUR per day ESTIMATE
- Daily rent: **35 EUR**
- Our margin: 21.84 EUR per day, or 5,678 EUR per year per van on 260 rental days
- Payback: immediate. We lease, we rent, we keep the difference.
- Zone it serves: Zone 2 and Zone 3, including between towns
- Licence needed: full licence, full KYC

### Why the van is leased and not bought

Buying a Ford E-Transit Courier is 30,640 EUR. Leasing it costs about 13.16 a day. We rent it at 35. So:

- We never tie up 30,640 of capital in a van
- We take no depreciation risk
- We earn 5,678 a year per van from day one

This is the single most important financial decision in the model. Lease the vans, buy the bikes.

## PART 4 - BREAK-EVEN DROPS PER TIER

**What this section is:** the number that decides whether renting is worth it for the courier.

| Tier | Rent per day | Drops to cover rent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cargo e-bike | 12 EUR | 2.0 drops |
| Electric moped | 20 EUR | 2.2 drops |
| Electric van, Zone 2 | 35 EUR | 3.9 drops |
| Electric van, Zone 3 | 35 EUR | 2.5 drops |

**Why this matters:** rent is affordable because the break-even is tiny. Two to four drops a day and the vehicle has paid for itself. Everything after that is the courier's money.

This should be shown IN THE APP, on the rental screen. A courier should see "3.9 drops covers your van today" before they rent.

## PART 5 - THE ADVERTISE-READY PRICES

**What this section is:** the exact lines to put in front of a prospective courier.

> **Ride with Kingdom Organics**
>
> **Cargo e-bike - 12 EUR a day.** No licence. No insurance. No experience needed.
> **Electric moped - 20 EUR a day.** AM licence required.
> **Electric van - 35 EUR a day.** Full licence.
>
> All inclusive: vehicle, charging, insurance where required.
> No deposit. No minimum days. Cancel any day.
> **You keep 100 percent of every delivery fee.**

Two audiences, two hooks:

**Already a courier elsewhere?** You are on 14 to 30 percent commission with Just Eat or Deliveroo. Here you keep the full fee and rent the vehicle for less than the commission you give away now.

**Never done this before?** Start on the cargo e-bike. No licence, no insurance, no test. Two drops pays for the bike that day. You can be earning by lunchtime.

**What this section is:** whether there are enough couriers in Tralee and Killarney to run the service.

Based on the two-hub population of 40,579:

| Pool | People |
| --- | --- |

| Would ever consider delivering, 4 pct | 1,055 |
| Flexible-work seekers, 15 pct | 3,956 |

**Launch need: 3 vans plus 6 bikes, so 9 couriers.**

The pool is over a hundred times the need. Recruitment is not the constraint here. The constraint is order volume.

| --- | --- |

## PART 9 - WHAT NEEDS WIRING INTO THE APP

**What this section is:** the honest gap between this model and what the app does today.

ALREADY BUILT in the driver module:

- driver KYC and vehicle record
- runs, queue and claim
- distance tracking
- fee table and payouts
- an existing driver fees admin screen with walk, e-bike, moped and car rates

NOT YET BUILT, and needed for this model:

1. A rental product in the courier sign-up flow, with the three tiers and daily prices.
2. A break-even indicator showing drops needed to cover the day's rent.
3. Distance bands in the fee table, replacing the flat rates for the van tier.
4. A rental ledger: who has which vehicle, which days, what they paid.
5. A lease-versus-buy decision record, so the van route is documented.

This is wire-up work on an existing module, not a rebuild. Nothing here requires a new app.
