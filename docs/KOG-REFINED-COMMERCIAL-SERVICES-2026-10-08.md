## 2. SERVICES - AURA RECEPTIONIST AND LIVE AUTO-BOOKING

### Aura receptionist

Already productised. The AI voice receptionist answers the shop phone, takes orders and answers questions when the owner cannot. Bundled as 300 minutes a month in the Complete plan, or sold standalone at 99 per month.

What it means for a shop: no missed call is a missed customer. The phone is answered evenings, weekends and during busy periods without hiring staff.

### Live auto-booking

Proposed addition. Customers pick a delivery window and the system books it live, with no human in between:

- a customer chooses a 2 hour window
- the system checks how many places are left in that window and books it
- the window is already built in the backend, with six daily windows and eight places each
- the courier sees the run appear automatically, no manual dispatch

Why it matters: a 15 minute drop supports a 15 to 30 minute promise. The big supermarkets sell two hour windows but miss them. We can hold the one promise we actually keep, confirmed instantly.
