## DECK 1 - STORE PARTNER PITCH (audience: independent grocers)

Slide 1 Title: Kingdom Organics. Fresh local and halal, delivered.
Slide 2 The problem: you pay 25-35 pct to Just Eat and Deliveroo, and they keep YOUR customer. On 2000 EUR of weekly orders that is 26000 EUR a year.
Slide 3 Our model, plain: we take 15 pct on a local order. Zone A up to 5km = 15 pct. Zone B 5-12km = 20 pct. Zone C 12km+ = 28 pct, plus a small customer delivery fee 3.99 / 5.49 / 6.99.
Slide 4 What you keep: your customer data, your shelf prices, your brand. We are a channel, not a landlord.
Slide 5 Founding pilot offer: first 3 Kerry stores get 8 pct for 12 months. Tralee first, 4-week Stage A. Own-car couriers, zero cost to you, no app build for you, we onboard your stock from photos in 24-48h.
Slide 6 Growth package (included free in year 1): AI inventory onboarding, live reporting dashboard, auto-invoicing, priority placement, 98-route backend. Value 2424 EUR.
Slide 7 The money maths: at 15 pct on a 50 EUR basket you pay 7.50 and a 3.99 customer fee; platform net is 4.44 per drop. Your saving vs a 25 pct aggregator on 2000/week is 20800 EUR a year, 22880 if you lock 12 months.
Slide 8 Trust and rules: no drip fees, no hidden auto-renewal, no commission bump after onboarding. GDPR - we never resell your data. You can leave any week.
Slide 9 Close: 3 pilot stores, 4 weeks, Tralee. Sign the founding rate, we bring you the digital customer you were losing.

## DECK 2 - RESTAURANT / FAST FOOD (audience: takeaways, 3 slides)

Slide 1 Title: Extra courier coverage, no extra commission.
Slide 2 Peak help: on your Friday/Saturday crunch we bring spare drivers so you stop turning away orders. Flat fee per drop, in-town 6.00, ring 9.00, between-town 14.00. No percentage cut, ever.
Slide 3 Why it is cheaper: on 20 peak drops a week at 6 EUR that is 120 EUR, against about 150 EUR of platform commission on the same orders - and you keep the customer. Saves roughly 1560 EUR a year and your brand stays on the bag.

## DECK 3 - COURIER RECRUITMENT (audience: drivers, own car)

Slide 1 Title: Drive with Kingdom Organics. Keep what you earn.
Slide 2 Your pay, honest: per drop, in-town 6.50, ring 10.00, between-town 15.50. You keep 100 pct of the driver fee we pay you - nothing deducted. (The customer delivery fee funds dispatch, insurance and support; it is not taken from you.)
Slide 3 Pilot safety net: minimum shift guarantee 40 EUR for a 3-hour booked window (about 13.33/hr floor). Above the floor you earn per drop. A worked week of 60 town, 20 ring, 10 long drops pays 680 EUR.
Slide 4 Own car is fine in the pilot: no van needed. Fuel is covered by the per-drop rate and the distance bands protect long runs.
Slide 5 Kingdom Electric Fleet, later: once funded you can rent a vehicle instead of your car. Cargo e-bike 9 EUR/day, no licence, no insurance. Electric moped 20 EUR/day, AM licence. Electric van 35 EUR/day, full licence. Pick the tier that suits your town.
Slide 6 Flexibility: book the shifts you want, paid weekly, no minimum commitment, stop any week.
Slide 7 Close: grab a 3-hour Tralee window this month. Guaranteed floor, real per-drop upside.

## DECK 4 - FUNDER / LEADER + LEO (audience: grant assessors)

Slide 1 Title: Kingdom Organics - rural grocery logistics, Tralee pilot.
Slide 2 What we ask: LEADER Analysis and Development, up to 90 pct aid, 30000 EUR cap, to fund the 3-store Tralee Stage A validation and the local demand study. Match needed about 3000 EUR. Kerry LEO Feasibility Grant 50 pct up to 15000 EUR, no match required.
Slide 3 The market: 7 Kerry towns, 54279 people, two hubs Tralee 26079 and Killarney 14500 = 74.7 pct of addressable demand. Independent grocers are losing online sales to 25-35 pct aggregators.
Slide 4 Why it is viable and safe: asset-light, zero-fleet Stage A, own-car couriers. The pilot SELF-FUNDS at about 6 drops a day with no capital - 4.44 net per drop on a 50 EUR basket. The grant buys SPEED and evidence, not survival. A project that cannot die if you say no.
Slide 5 The tech exists: FastAPI 98 routes, React PWA, 3 role pathways, AI inventory onboarding, open-source dispatch, 0 EUR vendor fees. Multi-tenant refactor is Phase 2.
Slide 6 Later green capital asks (not this stage): SEAI commercial EV 3800, VRT relief 5000 before 31 Dec 2026, ZEVI charging 5000 per charger. Fleet = Phase 3.
Slide 7 Roadmap: Phase 1 Tralee 3 stores. Phase 2 multi-tenant + Killarney Stage B. Phase 3 EV vans + e-bikes + depot solar + farm-to-door. Phase 4 22 stores.
Slide 8 Public value: keeps spend local, supports Kerry producers and halal community, low-emission last mile, rural jobs at a guaranteed floor.
Slide 9 Honest caveats on file: min-wage citation, real van lease and insurance quotes, BYD service network - all flagged, not hidden.
Slide 10 Close: fund the study, we bring the evidence and the first 3 stores live in 4 weeks.
