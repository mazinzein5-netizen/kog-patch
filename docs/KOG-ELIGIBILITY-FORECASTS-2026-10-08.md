## 1. FUNDER ELIGIBILITY - LEADER (NEWKD, Kerry)

Programme runs 2023 to 2027. Rates and ceilings as published.

| Type | Highest rate | Highest grant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investment in a business | 75 percent | 200,000 |
| Analysis and development, e.g. a feasibility study | 90 percent | 30,000 |
| Training | 100 percent | 200,000 |

Smallest grant 1,250. A Local Action Group may set its own minimum up to 5,000.

### What counts as eligible

For a business, the headings are: enterprise development, rural tourism and recreation, rural food production, farm diversification, the green economy, and social, community and cooperative enterprises.

### Exclusions that matter here

- conventional retail, EXCEPT community shops, farm shops and outlets selling locally produced goods
- cars, vans, buses, tractors and trailers
- working capital, including stock
- running costs such as wages, overheads and insurance
- primary farming and horticulture

So under LEADER the delivery fleet and grocery stock are not fundable. The market research and feasibility study is.

### Conditions

- at least 5 percent of total project cost in cash
- show the project could not go ahead without the grant
- show it will not take trade from a similar business nearby
- operate as funded for five years after the last payment
- expression of interest first, mandatory
- the Local Enterprise Office is named as the first stop for a starting business, and enterprise projects may be referred there before assessment
- no fee may be charged to applicants

## 2. FUNDER ELIGIBILITY - KERRY LEO, FEASIBILITY GRANT

Funds market research, consultancy, and prototype development to test an idea. Kerry is in the Southern and Eastern region.

| Rate | Cap |
| --- | --- |
| 50 percent of eligible cost | 15,000 |

Eligibility follows the national LEO rules for small businesses, generally up to 10 employees, including linked enterprises when counting for thresholds.

## 3. MARKET FORECASTS - EXTERNAL SOURCES

### Ireland, online grocery

- 2025 revenue about 237 million USD, with a 10 to 15 percent change projected for 2026. Source: ecdb.com, Online Grocery Industry in Ireland 2018-2030.

### Ireland, ecommerce overall

- 2025: 6.31 billion USD
- 2026: 6.83 billion USD
- 2031: 10.12 billion USD
- CAGR 8.2 percent, 2026-2031. Source: Mordor Intelligence.
- B2C ecommerce: 10.5 percent annual growth to 46.45 billion USD by 2026, then 7.8 percent CAGR to 2030. Source: Research and Markets.

### Global, online grocery

Sources diverge, so the honest range is what to use.

- 18.5 percent CAGR to about 1.82 trillion USD by 2030 (Heimdall Insights), or
- 21.3 to 25.5 percent CAGR to about 1.72 trillion USD by 2030 (The Business Research Company, Knowledge Sourcing).

### Global, quick commerce

- 2024: 111 billion USD
- 2030: about 353 billion USD
- CAGR 21.3 percent. Source: Research and Markets Quick Commerce report.

### The gap

No public forecast exists for the Kerry or Tralee share of online grocery. That local number is exactly what a market research or feasibility study would produce, and it is the kind of work both the LEO Feasibility Grant and LEADER Analysis and Development fund.
