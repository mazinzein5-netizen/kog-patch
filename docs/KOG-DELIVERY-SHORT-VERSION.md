# Kingdom Organics - Delivery Model, Short Version

Date: 2026-10-08. One page. Full detail in the two companion documents.

---

## 1. THE AREA

Tralee 26,079 plus Killarney 14,500 = **40,579 people, 75 percent of the market**.

Other towns: Listowel 4,800, Castleisland 2,500, Kenmare 2,400, Killorglin 2,200, Dingle 1,800.

Total area: 54,279 people.

---

## 2. THE THREE ZONES

| Zone | Where | Distance | Drops per 8h shift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 In town | inside Tralee or Killarney | 5 km | 22.9 |
| 2 The ring | Castleisland, Farranfore, Killorglin | 12 km | 16.0 |
| 3 Between towns | Tralee to Killarney, Listowel, Dingle | 30 km | 10.7 |

Drop time is 15 minutes. Cycle time is 21, 30 and 45 minutes.

---

## 3. THE PROBLEM

We pay a van courier a flat 9.00 per delivery. Their fuel is 0.14 per km.

A Tralee to Killarney round trip is 60 km = **8.40 in fuel against a 9.00 fee**.

At the flat fee the three zones pay the courier:

| Zone | Per hour | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 In town | 22.71 | good |
| 2 The ring | 14.30 | just above minimum |
| 3 Between towns | **6.90** | below minimum wage |

The rural courier is the one we underpay. So the rural customer is the one we fail.

---

## 4. THE FIX - DISTANCE BANDS

| Band | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Zone 1, under 5 km | **6.00** |
| Zone 2, 5 to 15 km | **9.00** |
| Zone 3, 15 to 35 km | **14.00** |

Never a flat 9.00 again.

---

## 5. VEHICLES - THE SPLIT IS FORCED

A pedal-assist cargo bike is capped at 25 km/h, about 20 km/h loaded.

| Zone | Hours driving | Hours left | Works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3.0 | 5.0 | yes |
| 2 | 5.0 | 3.0 | yes |
| 3 | 9.0 | -1.0 | **impossible** |

Zone 3 needs nine hours of driving in an eight hour shift. So:

**Bikes own Zones 1 and 2. Vans own Zone 3.**

---

## 6. TWO DEPOTS, NOT ONE

Serving Killarney from Tralee wastes 1.5 hours per cross-town drop.

| Cross-town drops per day | Hours lost | Share of shift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 4.5 | 56 pct |
| 6 | 9.0 | 112 pct |
| 10 | 15.0 | 188 pct |

Six drops is more than a full shift of driving. **One van per town, not one van.**

## 8. THE COURIER RENTAL LADDER (all electric)

| Tier | Rent per day | Licence needed | Cost to us |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cargo e-bike | **12** | none, no insurance | 1,449 buy |
| Electric moped | **20** | AM licence | about 3,000 buy |
| Electric van | **35** | full licence | 13.16 lease |

**The courier keeps 100 percent of the drop fee. Rent is the only deduction.**

Lease the vans, buy the bikes. Leasing a van at 13.16 and renting it at 35 earns 5,678 a year per van from day one, with no capital tied up.

## 9. THE ONE NUMBER THAT SELLS IT

Drops needed to cover a day's rent:

| Tier | Drops |
| --- | --- |
| Cargo e-bike | 2.0 |
| Electric moped | 2.2 |
| Van, ring | 3.9 |
| Van, between towns | 2.5 |

Two to four drops and the vehicle has paid for itself. Everything after that is the courier's money. **Put this on the rental screen.**
