# Kingdom Organics - Master Plan V2
Distance-based grocery and takeaway delivery, Tralee pilot. 2026.

---

## 1. One commission, never changes
A single 3-zone model applies to BOTH groceries and fast food:

| Zone | Store commission |
|---|---|
| A up to 5 km | 15 pct |
| B 5-12 km | 20 pct |
| C 12 km+ | 28 pct |

The rate rises with distance, so a long run gives the store AND the driver more cash. The commission never moves, no pilot discount on the commission.

---

## 2. Customer fee, shown upfront
The customer pays ONE total of EUR 3.99, itemised at the basket:
- Delivery EUR 2.50
- Service fee EUR 1.49

Both lines visible before checkout. No stacking, no surprise at payment.

---

## 3. Unit economics (Zone A)
Net per drop = commission + 3.99 fee - driver pay - 0.55 card

- GROCERY: 50 basket, 7.50 comm + 3.99 - 7.00 driver - 0.55 = NET 3.94
- FAST FOOD: 40 basket, 6.00 comm + 3.99 - 5.50 driver - 0.55 = NET 3.94

Both markets net 3.94 per drop. Same margin, different volume: takeaway turns over more drops a day.

---

## 4. Driver pay and the floor
Zone-scaled driver bands, plus keep 100 pct of tips:
- Grocery 7.00 / 9.50 / 14.00 (heavy, cold, 8-12 km rounds)
- Takeaway 5.50 / 7.50 / 11.00 (light bags, 6 km, high frequency)

The floor is a BOOKING rule, not a wage we pay from our own pocket: we open a driver window only when enough orders are already booked to clear the minimum. Thin day = we do not open it. Nobody is paid for an empty shift.

---

## 5. Packages (for stores only)
We sell CAPABILITY, not a cheap tool. A small shop gets the multimodal inventory scan, live tracking and delivery that Wix or Shopify do not have - supermarket-grade online sales plus doorstep delivery.
- Growth and Enterprise packages
- The 50 pct off applies to the PACKAGE for pilot stores, NOT the commission
- Price stays unlisted until we get a real quote

---

## 6. The pilot: 3 grocery + 3 fast food, Tralee
4-6 weeks, Stage A only, asset-light, ZERO capital. No vans or e-bikes in the pilot (couriers use their own cars); fleet is Phase 3, grant-funded. Stores sign up FIRST, then the customer marketing runs.

---

## 7. The money
Per store per month, mature Tralee:
- 1 grocery store at 6 drops/day = EUR 709/mo net
- 1 takeaway at 10 drops/day = EUR 1,182/mo net

3+3 mature (about 48 drops/day): EUR 189/day = EUR 5,674/MONTH net. Minus about EUR 500 marketing = about EUR 5,174/month.

---

## 8. Marketing-gated 4-week ramp
- W1 about 21 drops/day = EUR 579/week (cold-start dip)
- W4 about 66 drops/day = EUR 1,820/week
- 4-week net about EUR 4,800, less about EUR 500 est marketing = about EUR 4,300

Store count is NOT the lever. Demand comes from marketing, not from signing more till screens.

---

## 9. Drivers
Grocery about 8 heavy drops a 3-hour window, takeaway about 12 light drops.
- W1: 2-3 part-timers
- W4 peak: about 3-4 part-timers
No standing payroll, shifts open only when booked.

Under the EUR 37,500 services threshold there is no VAT, a real edge on price. Model the cliff; crossing it cuts net about 30 pct. Time the registration.
