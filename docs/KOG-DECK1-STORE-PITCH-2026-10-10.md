# Kingdom Organics - Store Partner
Fresh, organic and specialty, delivered. Tralee pilot. 2026.

---

## The problem
You pay the big delivery apps 25 to 35 pct of every online order, and they keep the customer.

---

## Our model, one fair commission
A single 3-zone commission that never changes, for groceries and for takeaway alike:
15 pct within 5 km, 20 pct to 12 km, 28 pct beyond. The longer the run, the more the store and the driver both earn.

---

## What you actually keep
On 2,000 EUR of weekly orders against a 25 pct big delivery app:
- Them: 500 EUR a week
- Us at 15 pct: 300 EUR a week
You save about 10,400 EUR a year, and the customer stays yours.

---

## The customer sees one small fee
Total EUR 3.99, shown upfront: Delivery 2.50 plus a 1.49 service fee. One transparent line, no stacking, no checkout surprise. Families are not nickel-and-dimed.

---

## Growth and Enterprise packages, for stores
Supermarket-grade online sales, the multimodal inventory scan, live tracking and delivery - things Wix or Shopify do not do. Pilot stores get the package at 50 pct off. The commission is separate and never discounted.

---

## Pilot, Tralee Stage A
3 stores, 4 to 6 weeks. Asset-light, couriers in their own cars. No lock-in, cancel anytime, your data is yours.

---

## Drivers paid properly
Zone-scaled per drop, keep 100 pct of tips. Heavy cold grocery rounds pay more so it is worth their fuel. A guaranteed window, not a gig gamble.

---

## Your data, your rules
GDPR, we do not resell or retain private customer data beyond what delivery needs. You own the relationship.

---

## The close
Keep the customer, pay a fair flat commission, and gain a tool even the supermarkets would envy. First 3 Tralee stores get the package at 50 pct off.
